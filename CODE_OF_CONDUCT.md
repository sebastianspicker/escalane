# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone should be able to take part in Escalane respectfully and
constructively, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Focus feedback on the work. When you disagree, explain your reasoning.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, alarm data, or personal
  information.
- Consider good-faith feedback and help newcomers find their way around.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive
bad-faith conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not include conduct details in a public issue. Use the repository Security
tab to open a private report titled `Conduct report` when private reporting is
available. If it is unavailable, open a public issue requesting private contact
without including the report details. Maintainers handle conduct reports separately
from security vulnerabilities and will keep them confidential as far as
practical. They may issue warnings, edit or remove content, or block
participants as appropriate.

This policy applies in all project spaces. It also applies to public behavior
elsewhere when that behavior materially affects the project community.
