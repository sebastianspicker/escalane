# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in escalane to be respectful, constructive, and
welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
role.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Focus feedback on the work and explain technical disagreement clearly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, alarm data, or personal
  information.
- Accept good-faith feedback and help keep the project approachable.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive
bad-faith conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not include conduct details in a public issue. Use the repository Security
tab to open a private report titled `Conduct report` when private reporting is
available. If it is unavailable, open a public issue requesting private contact
without including the report details. Conduct reports are handled separately
from vulnerability triage. Maintainers will protect confidentiality as far as
practical and may warn, edit, remove, or block participants as appropriate.

This policy applies to all project spaces and to public behavior when it
materially affects the project community.
